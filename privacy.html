Privacy policy

DayFlow app · Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Version française

In short

  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers.
  • With an account, only the data you choose to share with a group or a shopping list is sent, so the other members can see it.
  • Completed or old shared data is deleted automatically from the server.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on this page.
  • The free version shows a Google AdMob ad.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

DayFlow is developed by Vayler, in Belgium, who is the controller of your data.

Contact: vayler.pro@gmail.com

2. Data that stays on your phone

Your tasks, habits, hydration, vehicles, maintenance, appliances, expenses, history, personal shopping list and settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to our servers unless you share them.

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone.

3. Data sent if you create an account (optional)

The account is only used to share DayFlow with other people (family, flatmates…). In that case, we process:

DataWhy
Email address and password (encrypted)To sign you in to your account
First nameTo show it to the members of your groups (“Completed by Julie”)
Groups and shared shopping lists, their members, invitationsTo know who shares what with whom
Data you choose to share: tasks, shopping items (and their photos, up to 3 per item), vehicles, appliances, maintenance, related history and expensesTo show and sync them for the other members
Group activity feed (“Julie completed…”)To let members know what changes
Random technical device identifier and modification datesTo correctly merge changes made on several phones

Legal basis: performance of the service you request by creating an account and sharing (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Group members see the data shared in that group and the first names of the other members. They never see your unshared personal data or your email address.

4. How long do we keep this data?

We keep as little data as possible. An automatic clean-up runs every night:

Data on the serverRetention
Completed shared tasks35 days after completion
Shared history95 days
Deleted items14 days (so every phone can catch up)
Activity feed60 days
Photos of a cleared or deleted itemDeleted the next day
Invitations7 days after the reply, 30 days if unanswered
Deleted groups and their content30 days
Shared data in use (open tasks, shopping lists, vehicles…)As long as it is used in the group
Account (email, first name)Until the account is deleted

Your data always remains available on your phone, even after it is deleted from the server.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts accounts and shared data on servers located in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google AdMob shows the ad banner in the free version. Google may use device identifiers to serve and measure ads, according to the choices made in the consent form, which you can change in the app's Settings (“Ad choices”). See policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for targeted advertising.

6. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored encrypted. The server checks that only members of a group can read its shared data.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access, correct and export your data, object to its processing or ask for it to be deleted.

  • Export: Settings → Data → Export, in the app.
  • Change your first name: Settings → Group and sync.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Group and sync → Delete my account, or on the Delete my account page.
  • Erase all data from the phone: Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app.

For any other request: vayler.pro@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

8. Children

Creating an account is reserved for people aged 13 and over. Without an account, anyone can use the app, as its data stays on the phone.

9. Changes

This policy may change as the app evolves. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
