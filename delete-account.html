Delete my DayFlow account

You can delete your account and your data at any time. · Version française

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password and first name.
  • Your membership of all your groups and shared shopping lists.
  • Groups where you were the only member, with their content.

What is kept

  • Data you shared stays in groups that have other members, as it belongs to them too. Your first name is no longer shown (“Former member”).
  • Data stored on your phone stays there: to erase it, use Settings → Reset all data or uninstall the app.

Option 1: in the app (easiest)

In DayFlow: Settings → Group and sync → Delete my account, then confirm.

Option 2: here, without the app

Sign in with the email and password of your DayFlow account, then confirm the deletion.

Signed in as .

Option 3: by email

Write to vayler.pro@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my DayFlow account”. Deletion is done within 30 days at most.
